## A. Room-scale moved the capsule with the engine's teleport (10-11 to 10-19)

### A.1 What runs on a turn

Each frame at `ArkPlayer::Update` entry, `BodyFollow::Tick` (game thread):
1. **The body turn.** The torso yaw relaxes towards the head at 0.6/s, so after any turn the body
   is turned a little on most frames. The status line showed `applied` at 74–94% of `ticks` in
   every 10-18 run. Each turn calls `SetViewRotation`, which calls the entity's `SetRotation`.
2. **Room-scale.** When the follow point is more than 3 cm from the capsule, the follower glides
   the capsule towards it with `SetPos`. Up to PreyVR-42 it stood aside only when the capsule
   moved faster than 0.25 m/s since the last frame (`nativeMoving`).

### A.2 The engine treats a room-scale move as a teleport (proved)

The chain:
- `CEntity::SetPos` (`0x90AF40`) → transform update `0x905480`.
- That update sends the XFORM event unless `whyFlags` has bit 18 (`0x40000`, NO_EVENT).
- The physics component's handler (`0x96BE00`) builds `pe_params_pos` with only `pos`, and
  `bRecalcBounds = 1` hard-coded. It calls the living entity's `SetParams` (`0xB49610`, slot
  `+0x20` of `0x1D81580`).

Inside `SetParams`, if the position moved by more than 1% of the collider's half-height (`+0x1CC`,
a few mm), and `bRecalcBounds & 0x20` is clear:

```
B49A52  call 0xB49390            ; release the ground colliders (+0x2C8, +0x2D0)
B49A57  or   [rbx+2C0h],1        ; airborne
B49A76  mov  [rbx+16Ch],rsi      ; velocity = 0 (skipped only for entity 0x7777 with dynamics flag 8)
B49A7D  mov  [rbx+168h],esi
B49A83  mov  [rbx+1D0h],esi      ; step-smoothing offset = 0
```

**Requested velocity (`+0x174`) is kept, so the walk does not stop dead.** It has to accelerate
again from zero through the living entity's inertia, under air control until it lands.

**Body turning is not the stall.** `SetRotation` sends only `q`, so a yaw-only quaternion skips
that branch. The only rotation branch is a tilt correction, which a level quaternion does not
enter. Elsewhere on the XFORM event:
- ArkPlayer's handler (`0x15806E0` case 0) copies the rotation and runs the level-bounds check.
  It does not reset movement.
- The animated character (`ArkPlayer+0x1808`, handler `0x4F87A0`) re-syncs its location and zeroes
  its stored prediction translation on every XFORM whose `whyFlags` lack `0x1002000` (user or
  physics step). That includes each body turn. No effect on movement was found.

### A.3 Turning made room-scale move (proved by simulation)

The follow point (`roomscale::FollowPoint`) is the neck plus 8.05 cm along the *torso's* facing, so
that the native eye sits under the eyes when the head faces the torso. The torso yaw lags the head
(35° deadzone) and then relaxes towards it, so after a physical turn the follow point swings round
the neck. The real body has not moved.

`follow_sim.py`: head turning about a fixed neck at 90 fps, using the mod's settings.

| Turn | `SetPos` frames | Capsule moved | Over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 45° in 0.4 s | 16 | 5.3 cm | 1.1–4.0 s |
| 90° in 0.5 s | 29 | 10.0 cm | 0.3–1.9 s |
| 180° in 1 s | 74 | 22.5 cm | 0.3–2.4 s |
| 60° glance held 1 s | 17 | 5.9 cm | 0.2–5.8 s |
| 30° glance held 2 s | 0 | 0 | |

A snap turn pivots the origin about the eyes. When the head is turned away from the torso, the snap
shifts the follow point's offset by up to about 3.7 cm, which can start a glide too.

### A.4 Why the stand-aside guard did not keep these out of a walk

`nativeMoving` measured the capsule's speed after the fact, so the stall fed itself:
1. A glide is pending from a turn.
2. On the first frames of a walk, or after any hitch, the capsule is still slow, so the guard is
   open.
3. `SetPos` zeroes the velocity, so the capsule stays slow.
4. The guard stays open and the glide continues, frame after frame, until it is within 0.5 cm.

The third room-scale stage (10-11) cut the number of moves (follow the neck, not the eyes) and added
this guard. That fits "not as bad as before".

### A.5 The fix: move the capsule without the teleport (fix 1, 10-19)

**The idea.** Before the entity's `SetPos`, send the physics `pe_params_pos{pos}` with
`bRecalcBounds = 0x21`:
- bit `0x20` skips the living branch, and the base `SetParams` (`0xB88AA0`) still recomputes the
  bounds (proved);
- the entity's own sync then finds no position change, so it skips the branch too;
- `ChangeRequest` queues both in order if the physics step is running.

**What the static work changed** (contracts in `include/preyvr/RoomScaleNative.h`, checked by
`tools/re/verify_room_scale_physics.py`):
- **The teleport has a job at rest.** The living step only runs on a body that is moving, airborne
  or climbing a step. The teleport's airborne flag is what makes the next step settle a body moved
  while standing still onto the floor, ramp or step under it. So a move skips the teleport only
  while the body is moving: its own velocity (`pe_status_living` `+0x30`) or the movement's request
  (`+0x3C`) above 5 cm/s. At rest the teleport costs no velocity and is kept.
- **A move must not undo a physics step.** The change request (`0xB1BCD0`) applies a `SetParams` at
  once only while the world's step lock (`+0x576C`), `+0x57B0` and its lock (`+0x5784`) are clear
  and the body has nothing queued (`+0x40` below `0x20000`). Otherwise it is replayed after the
  step, where an absolute position computed before the step would put the body back by that
  step's movement.

**What each room-scale move does** (`RoomScaleMove.cpp`):
1. **Reads the body.** `pe_status_living` gives its velocity, the requested velocity and camOffset.
2. **Waits out a step-up.** While camOffset is non-zero (a step is being smoothed), no move.
3. **Tests the fit.** This is fix 2, the stance test for leaning, in
   [TURN-LEAN-ARMS-2026-10-19.md](TURN-LEAN-ARMS-2026-10-19.md). The two shipped together:
   without the teleport's airborne flag, an overlap is no longer pushed out at once, and would pop
   when the body next moved.
4. **Moves.**
   - If the body is moving, and only while the physics is idle (`PhysicsIdle`), it moves from the
     body's physics position (`+0xA8`): first `pe_params_pos{pos}` with `bRecalcBounds 0x21`
     (`keptMotion`), then the entity's `SetPos`. If the physics is busy, it waits a frame
     (`deferredBusy`). If the entity was behind its physics (`bodyLagMaxMm`), that catch-up is the
     walk's own movement: the view shows it rather than cancelling it.
   - At rest: the entity's `SetPos` alone (`restMoves`).

**Switches:**
- `body.physics 0` restores the rays and the plain `SetPos`, for A/B.
- `body.standaside 0|1` (see A.6).

**Tests.** `room_scale` covers the moving test: rest, a crawl, the stick before the body has sped
up, coasting and falling.

**Dropped from the first draft: gating room-scale on stick intent.** It would hold the body back
while the stick is used. Your requirement (10-19) is that the body keeps moving with you as a real
body does: no deferring room-scale while the stick is in use, no large step deadzone, no turning
room-scale off. Fix 1 removed the reason for it.

### A.7 Two leftovers, dealt with 10-24

The fix held. Run `player-20261024-145830`: `keptMotion=3004`, `deferredBusy=599`,
`bodyLagMaxMm=59`, `pushOut=17`, `viewDrift=0`. Two small gaps remained:

1. **A walk's first frames read as "at rest".**
   - `BodyMoving` reads the body's velocity and requested velocity, both from the previous frame's
     movement. On the first frame of stick input, a pending move took the plain `SetPos`, the
     teleport.
   - That costs no speed at rest, but it marks the body airborne and releases the ground, so the
     walk's first step accelerated under air control: a softer start, not a stop.
   - **Built:** a stick pushed within 150 ms (`kWalkStartNs`, `MoveLaneStickStarted`) counts as
     moving, so the move keeps the motion (`startKept`).
2. **A physics step between the two calls** (`pe_params_pos` 0x21, then the entity's `SetPos`).
   - The second call is then queued behind the step and replayed with the old position: the body
     goes back by that step, and takes the teleport.
   - The 10-19 work accepted this risk. The physics was busy for 17% of moves made mid-walk
     (`deferredBusy` 599 of 3,603), so the race was possible, if rare.
   - **Built:** a counter only. A move whose entity `SetPos` left the body with a change queued
     (`+0x40` at `0x20000` or more) counts as `raced`. Nothing changes until the count says it
     matters.

PreyVR-72: `startKept=6`, `raced=0`.

## B. Prey overwrote the mod's stick (10-24)

### B.1 How the mod drives movement and turning

- `UpdateMoveLane` posts the left stick as `xi_thumblx` / `xi_thumbly` events. The smooth-turn
  lane posts `xi_thumbrx`. Both go through `IInput::PostInputEvent` (`0x9D6D30`), in the
  `CSystem::Render` hook (`MoveLane.cpp`).
- **Each axis is posted only when its value changes** by more than 0.002 (`StickAxis`,
  `changeEpsilon`; the turn lane's own 0.002). A stick held still is not posted again.
- Prey's handlers store the values in `ArkPlayerInput` (inside `ArkPlayer` at `+0x8E0`):
  - move X `+0x5C` (`0x158FD20`) and move Y `+0x60` (`0x158FD80`);
  - yaw `+0x4C`, stored negated (`0x158FE50`).
- The mod's events carry no device symbol, on purpose (`InputEvent.h`). `PostInputEvent` then skips
  its symbol bookkeeping (`0x9D6E0D`). **So the gamepad's own input symbols never hold the mod's
  stick.** They hold whatever a physical pad reports, which is 0 when none is in use.

### B.2 What Prey does on an action-map event (proved)

`ArkPlayerInput` is also an action-map event listener. Its handler is `0x1590390`, slot 1 of the
vtable at `+8` (`0x1E580F8`).

- **Event 3, a map was enabled.** The handler checks `lparam != 0` and that the map is named
  `player`.
- **Event 2, a filter changed.** The handler checks that the input's mode stack is not empty and
  that its top is mode 0 (`player`). That is ordinary play.

Either way it calls `0x158E780`, which re-reads four axes and stores them:

| Action (table `+offset`) | Stored to | Instruction |
|---|---|---|
| `xi_movex` (`+0x2F0`) | `+0x5C` | `0x158E99A` |
| `xi_movey` (`+0x2F8`) | `+0x60` | `0x158EA03` |
| `xi_rotatepitch` (`+0x310`) | `+0x44`, negated | `0x158EA85` |
| `xi_rotateyaw` (`+0x308`) | `+0x4C`, negated | `0x158EAC6` |

Each read is `0x158EB90`:
1. If the action map manager says the action is filtered (slot `+0x1A8`), return 0.
2. Otherwise call `IInput` slot `+0xD8`, which is `LookupSymbol(gamepad = 3, pad index, key)`. The
   pad index comes from `CUIManager+0x3C`.
3. Return `SInputSymbol::value` (`+0x1C`).

So the re-read took the **physical pad's** stick. With VR controllers it read 0, and **zeroed
movement and yaw**. The cinematic gate (`+0x94`) is applied after the read, as in the handlers. The
same pass re-tests the sprint flag (`+0xAC`). It clears it when the stick reads centred, so
**sprint dropped** as well.

### B.3 What sends those events (proved)

**Event 3** is sent by `EnableActionMap` (manager slot `+0xD8`, `0x3CF050`), only when a map
actually changes state.
- `ArkPlayerInput` keeps a stack of input modes (`+0x10`..`+0x18`, 8-byte entries), with `player`
  (mode 0) at the bottom. The other modes are `world_ui_examination`, `examination`, `menu`,
  `flycam`, `focusmode`, `etherDuplicate`, `hacking`, `psi_scanning_fanfare` and `mimic_grab`.
- Popping a mode disables its map and re-enables the one below (`0x158F150`, `0x158EEC0`).
- So **returning to play from any other mode fires a re-read:** the weapon wheel (`focusmode`),
  keypads, screens and examining objects, notes and transcripts (they push `menu`), menus, hacking
  and the psychoscope.

**Event 2** is sent by `CActionFilter::Enable` (filter vtable `+0x38`, `0x3C3770`).
- It **broadcasts on every call, even when the filter's state does not change** (`0x3C3853`).
- The manager's `EnableFilter` (`0x3CF1C0`) calls it and then broadcasts again: two re-reads.
- Prey's filters are loaded from XML. Code toggles the ones it keeps as objects: the weapon wheel
  closing (`0x1381E40`), psi ability prompts (`0x15B65A0`), psi casting (`0x15B5D90`), game
  time-scale pause (`0x124D840`), door pry (`0x14517F0`), the death and load menus (`0x165AFF0`),
  the notes UI (`0x1633630`) and the game-rules freezes (`0x17421C0` and others). Level flow
  graphs and Lua can toggle filters by name too.
- **In the field** (PreyVR-72), what fired mid-walk was mostly `ArkPlayerInteraction`, the
  interaction prompt, then the inventory previews. See "PreyVR-72 in the headset".

Two more paths zero the axes:
- An explicit reset (`0x158EDE0`), called only on pause transitions and door prying.
- The cinematic gate.

### B.4 Why it looked random, and why strafing and smooth turning showed it most

1. A trigger fires and the player's axes become 0. The player slows to a stop.
2. The mod still believed the engine held its value, and posted again only when the stick moved by
   more than 0.002.
3. **A stick held hard against its rim gives a steady value.** Movement is clamped to the unit
   circle (`ShapeStick`), and smooth turning uses `|x|`, which saturates at 1.0. So nothing was
   re-posted and the stop lasted until the stick was nudged, as you found.
4. Smooth turning stopped with it, because yaw `+0x4C` is zeroed by the same pass.
5. The main trigger, the interaction prompt, switches as the view moves onto and off usable
   objects. Strafing and smooth turning both sweep the view across them.

## C. The mod's own zero posts (10-24)

`UpdateMoveLane` posted zero on both axes on any frame it had no input. The turn lane did the same.
- **Refused or stale tracking read.** `TryGetTrackingFrame` fails while the XR thread holds the
  write lock, or when the sample is over 200 ms old. The next frame re-sent the value: a one-frame
  dip, not a stop. The same read in `BodyFollow::Tick` missed 0 times in 58,186 ticks (`noHead=0`).
- **"Menu open" for even one frame.** `HudGameplayInputAllowed()` going false (any of the nine
  watched Danielle elements visible, or VR Options owning input) latched `awaitNeutral`, and
  `turnBlocked` for turning. Movement and turning were then held at zero until the stick came back
  within 0.15 of centre. After a real menu that is deliberate; a one-frame flicker would look
  exactly like a random stop.
- Head untracked or looking straight up or down, and a recentre, also released the stick. The first
  two only apply with head-relative movement.

## Ruled out

- **Body turning itself** (A.2): a yaw-only rotation skips the teleport.
- **The menu-state stamp going stale.** It is refreshed every frame, just before the move lane.
- **The controller-prompts scheme guard** calling Prey's scheme setter: 0 corrections in all 14
  runs from 10-23 to 10-24.
- **The explicit reset** (`0x158EDE0`). Only pause transitions and door prying call it.

## The 10-24 fix as built

PreyVR-72. Steps 1 and 2 are the cure for B; 3 closes C; 4 is A.7; 5 makes every run show the
evidence.

**1. Prey's re-read finds the mod's stick** (`StickReread.cpp`; contracts in
`include/preyvr/StickRereadNative.h`):
- Hooks `0x158EB90`, the per-axis read; only the re-read calls it (four calls). For `xi_movex`,
  `xi_movey` and `xi_rotateyaw` it returns what the lane delivered (`MoveLaneHeldMilli`), which is
  what a real pad's symbol would hold. The re-read stores it in the same call: no stop, no
  one-frame dip, and sprint is kept.
- It keeps the pad's value when the lane holds nothing, when a real pad is pushed, when the manager
  says the action is filtered (asked as the engine asks), or when the manager cannot be asked. The
  caller still applies the cinematic gate.
- It also watches the listener (`0x1590390`) to name the event and count it. Every re-read that
  would have stopped you is logged as
  `preyvr_stick result=0 detail=reread event=filter name=... enable=... heldMilli=x,y,yaw kept=1`
  (60 lines per session; the rest are counted as `unlogged`).
- `move.reread 0|1` (default 1). With 0, Prey's value is kept, and the counting and logging carry
  on, for A/B.
- Installed at VR start. Not fatal: if the contracts refuse, step 2 still restores the stick a frame
  later.
- **Considered and not chosen: posting the stick every frame.** Simpler, but the re-read can land
  after a frame's post and before the player's movement update, leaving a dip of up to a frame each
  time: hitches in place of stops.

**2. The per-frame read-back** (`MoveLane.cpp`, `ReassertHeld`):
- After each lane posts, it reads `ArkPlayerInput` from the player (`+0x8E0`, vtable checked).
- An axis that differs from what was delivered is posted again and read back: `reasserted` when
  restored; `reassertRefused` when still refused (a filter), not tried again for 250 ms.
- It skips axes the cinematic gate, or yaw's own block, legitimately holds at zero.
- The first 20 are logged as `preyvr_move result=0 detail=reassert axis=... storedMilli=... heldMilli=...`.
- **One place to watch.** Prey zeroes movement itself when a door pry starts (`0x158EDE0`), then
  switches the `DoorPry` filter. If that filter does not block movement, the read-back gives a held
  stick back during the pry, which a real pad would not do. A `detail=reassert` line during a pry
  would show it.

**3. The mod's own gaps:**
- **Refused or stale samples.** A refused or stale tracking read, or an untracked head when moving
  head relative, keeps the last stick for up to `move.hold` ms (default 100; 0 = as before). The
  turn lane does the same.
- **The menu flag** (`input::MenuDebounce`): it takes the sticks only after two frames. On its first
  frame both lanes hold what they sent. Each change of the flag is logged with the element that
  caused it: `preyvr_hud result=0 detail=menu_state open=1 elements=...` (200 per session).
  `menuFlickers` counts one-frame flags, which no longer stop you.

**4. Room-scale:** the walk start and the race counter, A.7.

**5. On the status line** (every minute), a `move={...}` section:
- `lane={...}`: `released={off menu read head recentre centre}` (held sticks let go, by reason);
  `bridged` and `turnBridged` (sample gaps held across); `menuFlickers` and `menuTook`;
  `reasserted` and `reassertRefused` per axis; `native`.
- `reread={...}`: `mapEvents`, `filterEvents`, `wouldStop`, `kept`, `filtered`, `padPushed`,
  `unasked`, `unattributed`, `unlogged`.
- `body={...}` gains `startKept` and `raced`.
- `move.report` prints the same at any time.
